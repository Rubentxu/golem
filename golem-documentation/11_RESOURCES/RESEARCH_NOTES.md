# Research Notes incorporated

## ActiveGraph patterns
Event-sourced graph, reactive behaviors, relation behaviors, pattern subscriptions, scoped views, patches/proposals, policies, replay, fork/diff/promote, packs e isolated sinks. GOLEM no depende de ActiveGraph; reimplementa ideas pertinentes en Go y para SaaS distribuido.

## Supply-chain standards
SLSA, SPDX, CycloneDX, in-toto, Sigstore y OCI se usan como standards/adapters, no como modelos propietarios.

## Tuleap
Behavioral reimplementation: conservar capabilities útiles, no trasladar arquitectura interna. Paridad final requiere discovery spike.
